How it works

It hears the room

AceCue captures your meeting tab's audio and transcribes it live — nothing is recorded or stored.

The question is detected

The moment the interviewer finishes asking, AceCue isolates the actual question from the chatter.

Your answer streams in

A response tailored to your résumé and the job appears in about a second. Glance, speak, move on.

Bring any model

Paste one key — AceCue detects the provider automatically.

Use it honestly

AceCue is a preparation and accessibility aid.

Practice Mode is always fair game.

Private by architecture

AceCue is built so your interview never leaves your hands.

API keys live only in your browser — never on our servers.

Audio is transcribed in real time and never recorded or stored.

Your résumé and job context stay on your device unless you opt into sync.
