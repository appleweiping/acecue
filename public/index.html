Practise the answer before the interview does.

Pick a role, get a question, and answer it out loud or in writing. You get a breakdown of the answer's structure, concrete things to fix, and the follow-up question an interviewer would likely ask next.

  • No key? The offline checker looks for each part of a STAR answer (Situation, Task, Action, Result), counts numbers, filler words and "we" versus "I". It is a heuristic, not an AI.
  • Have a key? Switch to AI model for questions based on your résumé and the job posting, and written feedback.
  • Just looking? Try a one-click demo fills in a sample résumé and answer and shows the feedback.

    Privacy. Your résumé, job description and answers stay in this browser (localStorage) unless you use AI mode. In AI mode, the text you submit and your API key are sent to this site's /api/chat function, which forwards them to the provider you picked and does not log or store them. Dictation uses your browser's speech service (in Chrome, that is Google).

    AceCue is for practice. It has no features for use during a real interview.
